# Content sources and originality

Every problem statement in `src/catalog.ts` was written for this project.
None of it reproduces or closely paraphrases a real past exam paper from any
exam board. Each track is modeled, in the author's own words, on the general
level and subject focus of a well-known Czech exam type — subject areas and
difficulty expectations are public knowledge, not copyrighted; the specific
problems, numbers, and wording are original.

- **`ss-prijimacky`** — modeled on the general level of the Czech jednotná
  přijímací zkouška (unified entrance exam) for admission from lower- to
  upper-secondary school: arithmetic with negative numbers, fractions,
  percentages, and single-variable linear equations, with gentler
  difficulty progression than the other tracks.

- **`vs-prijimacky`** — modeled on the general level of a Czech university
  entrance exam in mathematics: the same core topics as `ss-prijimacky`,
  plus harder linear equations with the variable on both sides.

- **`maturita`** — modeled on the mathematics portion of the Czech maturita
  (upper-secondary school-leaving exam): assumes the basic arithmetic is
  already solid, so it moves faster into equations with distribution and
  the variable on both sides.

- **`vysoka-skola`** — a foundational algebra refresher aimed at a
  first-year university student, covering the same linear-equation and
  fraction skills but framed as review rather than exam prep.

No text, problem, or image from any official exam board (including Cermat)
was downloaded, scanned, transcribed, or used as a basis for any statement
in this catalog.
